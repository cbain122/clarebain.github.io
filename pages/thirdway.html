Third Way

A brand identity I built in 2015, then returned three years later to help tell the organization's story to its most important audiences.

Client
Third Way
Services
Brand Identity, Web Launch, Presentation Design, Data Visualization
My Role
Brand Designer, Multimedia Designer
Year
2014–2018

The Challenge

Third Way is a Washington, D.C. public policy think tank whose entire value proposition rests on being seen as clear-eyed and pragmatic. When I joined as a Junior Designer in 2014, its visual identity didn't match that positioning: a detailed, ornate logo mark, a serif typeface, and a dated toolkit of drop shadows and gradients, all set to carry over into a new website the organization was preparing to launch.

My Role

I worked with Third Way across two stints under Senior Manager of Design Bill Rapp: first in 2014–2015, when I led a full rebrand and helped implement it on the organization's newly launched website; then again in 2017–2018 as Multimedia Designer, this time focused on the stakeholder-facing storytelling built on top of that system.

Brand System & Website Launch (2015)

I led the rebrand: a simplified logo mark, a modern sans serif typeface, and a minimal but impactful color palette, documented in a style guide adopted organization-wide, then helped carry that system into the site as it launched.

Proposals & Presentations (2017–2018)

Returning three years later, I designed the pitch decks and policy reports supporting campaigns, partnerships, and grant proposals, working within the system I'd built to keep every stakeholder touchpoint consistent.

Events & Activations

I designed materials for Third Way's events and partner activations, along with merch and swag extending the brand into physical touchpoints.

Editorial, Data Visualization & Web Content

I designed infographics translating dense policy topics, financial regulation, education policy, foreign affairs, into visuals a general audience could understand at a glance, formatted policy reports, and kept the website's content and visual consistency current.
