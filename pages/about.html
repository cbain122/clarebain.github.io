About

I'm a senior designer with 10+ years leading brand identity, digital campaigns, and UX across every channel. My career has moved between mission-driven organizations and fast-moving consumer brands, from leading a policy think tank's brand relaunch to running an always-on retail marketing engine today.

I work independently and remotely, managing multiple concurrent projects with minimal oversight, and I care as much about the system behind the work as the work itself: the guidelines, templates, and processes that let good design hold up at scale, long after I've moved on to the next thing.

Outside client work, I make illustration and fine art under the name Clarety, work that keeps my eye sharp and feeds back into everything I design for clients.
