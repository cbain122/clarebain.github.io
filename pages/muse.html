Muse Paintbar

A full rebrand and campaign program overhaul for a 25+ location paint-and-sip brand, plus a UX redesign that lifted bookings 8% in 45 days.

Client
Muse Paintbar
Services
Brand Identity, Digital Campaigns, UX, Web
My Role
Brand Lead, Campaign Strategist, Designer
Year
2023–Present

The Challenge

Muse Paintbar is a paint-and-sip studio brand with 25+ locations, running high-volume digital campaigns across every channel with a small, fully remote team. When I joined, the brand identity hadn't kept pace with the business: inconsistent color and type usage across locations, no unified style guide, and a fragmented email platform that couldn't support the scale the marketing calendar demanded. My job was to rebuild the brand from the ground up, then run the always-on campaign engine that identity now had to support.

My Role

I led brand and digital marketing for Muse end to end, owning everything from the visual identity system through the campaigns and product experience that used it, working independently and remotely with minimal oversight across 25+ retail locations.

Brand System

I led a full rebrand: a new color palette, typography system, and brand asset library, documented in a comprehensive style guide adopted across every location and channel: digital campaigns, social, print collateral, in-store signage, and email. I also designed merch and branded product lines for events and retail activations, extending the identity into physical touchpoints.

Campaign Program

I planned and executed 205 email and SMS campaigns in Klaviyo year-to-date, reaching 5.5M recipients. I led the full platform migration to Klaviyo and built the segmentation, flows, and templates that let the program scale without sacrificing consistency with the new brand system. I also produce animated graphics and GIFs for social, email, and SMS to keep the content feeling current across every send.

Web & Ecommerce

I manage visual consistency, content, and UX across the company website and Shopify ecommerce pages. I redesigned the private event booking pages and self-checkout flow, simplifying the path from inquiry to confirmed booking, a change that drove an 8% increase in bookings within the first 45 days. I also design presentation decks supporting investor outreach and partnership storytelling.
